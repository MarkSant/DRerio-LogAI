# F1 — time foundation: changelog fragment

To be merged by F7 into `CHANGELOG.md` under 8.0.0.

## Changed

- **ROI latency to first entry.** It used `bool.diff()`, which is an XOR: when the subject started
  inside an ROI, the first "change" was the EXIT, and the latency came out `None` next to an entry
  count of 1. Latency now uses the rising edge (`cur & ~prev`) and an explicit `t0` (default: the
  first row, as before). A subject already inside at `t0` gets latency `0.0` with
  `latency_present_at_start = True`; entries count only real outside→inside transitions after
  `t0`. Golden fixture: `latencia_primeira_entrada.Esquerda` goes from `None` to `0.0` (group
  and per animal); `contagem_entradas.Esquerda` stays 1 and the time in ROI is unchanged. ROIs the
  subject entered from outside (`Direita`, 2.1 s) do not move.
- **Inter-visit latencies** (center/periphery analysis) had the same XOR and listed every exit as
  an entry. They now list entries only.
- **`duracao_video_s` / `video_duration_s` (xlsx).** Was `len(df) / fps`, a ROW count: doubled with
  two tracks, divided by N with `analysis_interval_frames = N`, and wrong whenever the configured
  fps was not the video's. It is now `(t_last − t_first) + median_dt` over the unique timestamps.
  Example: 2 tracks × 100 frames at 10 fps went from 20 s to 10 s; every 10th frame of a 10 fps
  video went from 1 s to 10 s; a 10 fps video with settings at 30 fps went from 3.3 s to 10 s. The
  golden is unchanged (one track, every frame, fps right — the only case rows/fps got right).
- **Frame rate of reports.** Individual reports, parquet summaries and the pipeline reporter took
  `settings.video_processing.fps` (30) as the truth. They now take the recording's own rate from
  `analysis/time_base.resolve_time_base()` (`3c` heartbeat > frame ledger > trajectory PTS); the
  setting is only the fallback and, when used, adds a warning to the report.
- **Live re-analysis is re-timed from the frame ledger.** When a `6_FrameLedger_<base>` sits next
  to `3_CoordMovimento_<base>`, reports re-date the trajectory with the capture instant
  (`t_capture_perf − t0_perf`, joined on `pipeline_frame == frame`), in memory only. The live
  `timestamp` is a processing clock. If any row has no capture instant, the trajectory is left on
  its own clock with a warning rather than mixing two clocks.

## Added

- Report keys (English, always present, so the unified report never sees a schema mismatch):
  `analysis_window = {"status": "none"}`, `latency_censored` and `latency_present_at_start`
  (`{roi: bool}`, also per animal under `analise_roi.por_animal`). The existing Portuguese keys
  are untouched (persistence contract). The xlsx and the unified report are unchanged.
- `validacao.estatisticas.duration_s` next to `total_frames` (which stays a row count).
- `AnalysisService.run_full_analysis(..., window=None)`: the parameter is reserved for F4b; any
  non-`None` value raises `NotImplementedError` instead of being silently ignored.
- `ROIAnalyzer.get_latency_details(t0, t_end)` and `t0`/`t_end` on latency, entry counts, time in
  ROI and per-animal metrics. With `t_end` given and no entry, the latency is censored at
  `t_end − t0`.
